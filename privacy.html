Claude Quest – Privacy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Claude Quest is a free study game for a small group of friends. It is private and invite-only. It is not made by Anthropic.

What we store

  • From your Google account: your email address and a user ID. We never see your Google password.
  • The name you choose in the game and, if you add one, a profile picture (made smaller, with photo location data removed).
  • Your game progress: XP, streak, finished lessons and badges.

Who can see it

  • Other players who joined with the invite code see your name, picture, XP, streak, lessons and badges on the leaderboard.
  • Your email address and your detailed progress are visible only to you.
  • We do not sell or share your data. There are no ads and no tracking.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, in the EU (Frankfurt). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Delete your data

In the game, open Profile and tap “Delete my account data”. This removes your profile, progress and picture.
